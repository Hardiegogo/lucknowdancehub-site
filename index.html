01 / About

Built for practice.

Lucknow Dance Hub opened in 2018. A team of instructors trains students across a wide range of dance forms, from a first class to advanced choreography.

The work goes out on camera as well. Hundreds of choreography videos, workshop sessions and festival specials are filmed on the studio floor, which is how most people in the city find us.

Styles taught

  • Popping
  • Animation
  • Robot
  • Hip Hop
  • Bollywood
  • Bollywood Fitness
  • Kathak
