01 / His story

One studio, built on repetition.

Achal founded Lucknow Dance Hub in 2018 as a place in the city where dancers could train seriously across styles rather than pick up one routine and leave. He owns and manages the academy, leads its team of instructors, and choreographs across every level the studio teaches.

His own specialisation is popping, with animation and robot work layered on top. That control shows up in how the academy teaches street styles, and it runs through the choreography posted from the studio floor.
